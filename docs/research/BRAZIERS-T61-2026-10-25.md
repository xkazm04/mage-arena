# T61 the brazier flame reads as fire (option B), 2026-10-25

**Label: desktop proxy (D3D12, forward shading, `-nohmd`), not Quest truth.** Engine UE 5.8.3 (CL 58210709;
`Build.version` read before the first build). No combat number moved; nothing under `apps/vr/data/pinned/`,
`PINNED.json` or `conformance/` changed.

**Outcome.**

- **Option B is in code and under test.** The heart's luminance sits strictly above the rim's and strictly below
  min(Fire, Earth) in the greybox palette and styles 13, 25 and 30; the body sits in the same band, at or under the heart.
  The 0.15 chroma margin off water, fire, air, earth, steel, the rim, the core, `projectiles.fire` and `arena.flame` holds
  with at least 0.198 to spare (style 25's body off Earth). Steel, the known risk for a near-white heart, stays 0.54 or
  more away.
- **The near braziers left the unblockable's flight line.** `Greybox.Brazier.ClearOfUnblockable` (new) holds it: no
  brazier's screen box comes within the gap of the rim's disc anywhere on the demo flight from the centre seat. Every
  critic round read the old near-right brazier "directly under the black disc, its flame touching the red rim"; none
  read that of the new layout.
- **The flame reads as fire, but only weakly.** Each of the three fresh critics picked the new flame as the fire in
  every pair (10 / 10 each round) and called it "a pale candle or gas tuft, no orange body, no height". The card's
  acceptance (a) wants a plain YES; three rounds of colour tuning inside the band did not reach it (section 5).
- **A new crowding.** At (-3.5, +/-7.8), the near-right brazier stands behind the fire bolt and the near-left one behind
  the steel line in `04-threats-midflight`; all three critics marked that frame YES on (b) for crowding ("its pale flame
  pokes out at the bolt's edge"). It is a position finding; the rounds could only tune colour (section 6).
- **Cost: +0.** D-G4 max 126, as T60.

## 3. The new brazier position, and why

The demo unblockable starts far right and flies at the seat. From the centre seat, the near-right brazier at (-2, 7.2)
(+Y is the seat's right) stood under it for the whole flight; T60's critics read its flame "breaking the red ring" in
every round. c39f05b moved it to **(-3.5, 7.8)**: 1.5 m nearer the seat and 0.6 m further out, which puts it 3.4 degrees
clear of the rim's disc over the whole flight. `Greybox.Brazier.ClearOfUnblockable` checks this on screen boxes from the
seat at every point of the flight. Its partner mirrors to (-3.5, -7.8), keeping every prop pair symmetric. Both stay
inside the oval, off the centre flight line, clear of the banners (x 9.5), the crowd and the dais.

What the move did not foresee: in `04-threats-midflight`, the fire bolt now passes in front of the new near-right
brazier, and the steel line in front of the near-left one. The threats are drawn over the braziers (no threat is hidden),
but every critic read the flame showing at the bolt's edge as "a flicker of the bolt".

round's build. The pairs were greybox `01-initial-view` and `04-threats-midflight`, and T58 `04-fire-bolt`, `05-fire-absorb`,

## 6. Open

- **(a) is not met: the flame reads as fire only weakly.** Every critic asked for what option B's band forbids: "no
  orange body, no height", and S1's "tall, bright, saturated yellow-orange flames". Within the band, the heart is
  already at 0.95 of min(Fire, Earth), and a more saturated body walks into Earth's chroma (round 2). What is left is
  shape (a taller lick than T60's cone envelope, which T60 capped so it could not hide the rim) or option C (light: an
  emissive bowl rim or a sand light pool). Option C needs a ruling. Now that the near braziers are off the
  unblockable's line, the cone cap could be lifted, but that is a shape change outside this card's colour-only rounds.
  Recommendation: a ruling on a taller flame (the cone cap lifted now that `ClearOfUnblockable` guards the rim), measured
  against the same critic.
- **(b) crowding from the new position.** At (-3.5, +/-7.8), the fire bolt (right) and the steel line (left) of
  `04-threats-midflight` pass in front of the near braziers. No threat is hidden, but the flame at the bolt's edge reads
  as part of it. A position that clears the unblockable, fire and steel lines of the demo frames (for example nearer the
  wall, further back) would need the clearance test widened to every demo threat.
- **`MageArena.Overlay.Golden` is red** on the moved layout, on hash lines only (section 2.3). The re-record patch is
  ready (`runs/T61/overlay-golden-rerecord.patch`, or re-run with `-MageArenaRecordOverlayGolden`); it touches
  `apps/vr/data/scenarios-vr/overlay/`, outside this lane's paths.

  measure is still owed.
