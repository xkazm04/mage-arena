## 7. Decisions the card did not specify

- **Two seeds.** The card's seed 1 cannot show Death (section 4.1), so seed 2 follows it in the same run.
- **A cast during a Cast plays on; a hit during a Hit restarts.** "Damage event" is read as a `hit` event that removed HP
  (`Value > 0`). A hit absorbed to 0 is no Hit.
- **Death is his `down` event.** In the kernel that is his 0 HP, and it ends the duel ("a downed rival ends the duel",
  `VrRivals.cpp`).
- **The figure keeps his greybox HP bar** and hides only the column.
- **Card deliverable 1 is not done.** It asked for the import committed under `Content/Characters/Brennic/` with
  `A_Brennic_*` names and a `SOURCE.md`; the DINOv3 hold overrides it. The figure loads the FBX import's
  `SK_Brennic_Anim_<Clip>` names, and also takes `A_Brennic_<Clip>` if a committed import ever uses the card's names.
- **The runs/T38/ capture lives in staging, not in the repo.** The report is this file, not `runs/T38/REPORT.md`.
- The card's "5.8.3 simulator boot" note for `docs/XR-TOOLCHAIN.md` is out of this lane's paths and was not written.
