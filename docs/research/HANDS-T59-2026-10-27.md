# T59 Player hand mesh, 2026-10-27

**Label: desktop proxy (D3D12, forward shading, `-nohmd`), not Quest truth.** Engine UE 5.8.3 (`Build.version` read before
the first build). No combat number, nothing under `apps/vr/data/`, no clip, no asset file and no paid service. Evidence is
in `C:/Users/kazda/mage-art-staging/hands-t59-2026-10-27/`: `before/` and `after/` (the six greybox capture stills and
`budget.json` from each build), `after-v1/` (the first green look, before the palm was widened), and `logs/`.

## 1. What was built

- `apps/vr/Game/Source/MageArenaVR/Hands/HandMesh.h`, `HandMesh.cpp` (new):
  - `MageHandMesh::BuildPartMesh(EHandPart, Outer, Material)` builds a unit mesh in code. The mesh is a superellipsoid fitted
    exactly to the -0.5..0.5 box, 12 sides x 10 rings, 216 triangles, with smooth normals.
    - **Finger:** a round cross-section, straight sides, rounded ends, narrowing to 0.82 toward the tip.
    - **Palm:** a rounded-rectangle cross-section with no taper.

    The vertices are written straight into LOD0's render buffers (`FStaticMeshLODResources`), which is the engine's
    fast runtime build without an `FMeshDescription` (see section 6).
  - `MageHandMesh::PoseParts(FHandFrame, Out)` lays one part along each of the 25 bones of the frame:
    - 15 finger segments: 3 on the thumb and 3 on each finger.
    - 10 palm blocks: the wrist-to-palm block, the thumb's carpal block, and a carpal and a metacarpal block per finger.
      They fan from the wrist and overlap into one palm.
    - One forearm block behind the wrist.

    Each part is centred on its bone, with Z along the bone and Y (its thickness) on the palm normal. It runs 0.4 of its
    width past each joint, so the parts overlap and no seam opens on a bend. The proportions are chunky (VISUAL-PLAN (c),
    "big hands"): each finger is a little wider than its share of the clips' knuckle line (about 1.7 cm), so the fingers
    touch, as in S1.
- `Greybox/HandPresentationComponent.{h,cpp}`: the joint and bone layers became `Parts`, one instanced layer per
  `EHandPart`. `UpdateHands` reads `UHandInputSubsystem::GetLatest` for each side as before, calls `PoseParts` and adds the
  instances. The cast flash and the ward arc are unchanged. There is no new input path: the hands draw only what the
  active hand source (clip player or live) delivers.
- `Tests/HandMeshTests.cpp` (new): `MageArena.Hands.MeshNotSpheres` and `MageArena.Hands.MeshFollowsPose` (section 3).

Colour: the layout's `hand` tint (0.75 / 0.64 / 0.54, warm tan, the style block's "warm tan skin") on `BasicShapeMaterial`,
the same as before. It is not an element colour, not steel and not the black core with a red rim. S1's dark sleeve was
**not** added, because a dark block at the wrist could read toward the black-core class.

## 6. Decisions the card did not specify

- **Code-built geometry, not an authored asset.** Nothing goes under `Content/Hands/` and nothing through LFS. The meshes
  are built at BeginPlay, one pair per pawn (two meshes of 216 triangles). The ledger row is
  `docs/submission/ASSET-LEDGER.md` Group 10.
- **Render buffers filled directly.** `UStaticMesh::BuildFromMeshDescriptions` failed to link
  (`LNK2019 FMeshDescription::FMeshDescription`): the MeshDescription and StaticMeshDescription modules are not in
  `MageArenaVR.Build.cs`, and the card's paths do not include that file. Filling `FStaticMeshLODResources` (positions,
  tangents, one UV channel, 16-bit indices, one section) and calling `InitResources` uses only the Engine module. This
  follows the engine's own fast-build path, except that it skips the optional depth-only and reversed index buffers.
  **Not yet checked on a cooked Android build** (V1).
- **Articulated rigid parts, not a skinned mesh.** One deforming mesh per hand would need a dynamic or skinned component.
  A skinned hand needs a rigged asset (the VISUAL-PLAN pipeline, a later card), and a dynamic component (ProceduralMesh /
  GeometryFramework) needs Build.cs and `.uproject` changes. The rigid parts stay on the existing instanced path, at no
  extra draw call.
- **26 parts per hand on purpose.** Twenty-five bones plus the forearm keeps `Budget/BudgetStressDriver`'s drawn-hand rule
  (instances / `MageHandJointCount`) true without touching `Budget/`. `MeshNotSpheres` holds that floor.
- **The forearm block** (11 x 5 x 3.6 cm, skin tone) closes the wrist so the hand does not end in a cut.
- `arena-layout.json` `hands.jointRadiusM` and `boneRadiusM` are still parsed, but the hands no longer use them. Its
  `_note` still describes spheres. `apps/vr/data/vr/` is outside this card (see the questions in the run result).
