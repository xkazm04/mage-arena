## 2. The test, red then green

`MageArena.Greybox.Brazier.ClearOfThreatLanes` (`Tests/BrazierTests.cpp`, a sibling of T61's `ClearOfUnblockable`,
which stays) checks every brazier against every demo lane (water, fire, steel, unblockable) from the centre seat, at
every 1% step of each flight, whether the threat is in front of the brazier or behind it. Each check compares two screen
boxes. The brazier's box runs from its foot to the flame's top (`MaxFlameTopRadii`), plus or minus the bowl radius. The
threat's box is its body, or the rim's outer edge for the unblockable. The steel bar counts as a rod of `lengthM` along its
flight. The threat's box carries a 1 degree margin.

**One rule the brief did not spell out** is the backdrop rule. A brazier further from the seat than the far spawn ring
(15 m) counts as backdrop, like the wall and the crowd. It is not checked while the threat is inside the near spawn ring
(8 m), because a threat that close is large enough to pass in front of any backdrop. Without this rule, the
unchanged far pair fails in the last 12-17% of the unblockable's and the steel's flights, because the threat is then 3-6 m
from the seat. In `04-threats-midflight` the far pair is clear: every flight is between 18% and 90% there, and the
unblockable is at about 18%. The rule exempts only the far pair, and only in those last frames. The near pair is checked over
every point of every flight. Whether the far pair should move is left to Athena (section 8).
