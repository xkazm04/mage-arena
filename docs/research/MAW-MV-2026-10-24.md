## Which path won: split and merge

**The merge won,** for three reasons:

- the two-view mesh has no geometry to finish;
- the merge is the only path with a closed back;
- the merge keeps the accepted s7 front, its gape, teeth and tongue pocket, untouched.

No re-seed was run.

### The merge (`apps/vr/tools/mesh/merge_halves.py`, new; README "Split and merge")

```
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P apps/vr/tools/mesh/merge_halves.py -- <creature-mesh>/maw/s7/maw-s7.glb <out>/back-only/maw-back-s7.glb <out>/merge/try2/maw-merged.glb -50 315 -0.3 0.05 1.25
```

**Pose.** Both meshes go into finish.py's frame: facing -Y, feet at z 0, 1.25 m.

- The front s7 mesh is turned -50 degrees, CREATURE-MESH's rot, which puts the gape square to -Y.
- The back mesh is turned 315 degrees, so its s7301 side looks down +Y.
- The brief said "mirrored on Y"; the script **turns** the back mesh, it does not reflect it. A reflection would swap
  its left and right flanks and invert its winding, and the script refuses a reflecting pose.
- The back is scaled to the front's full height and its 1-99 percentile footprint (2.198 x 2.062 m). Its scale is
  x 2.806, y 2.687, z 2.691 (anisotropy 1.045); the front's is 2.760.

**Cut.**

- Each half is bisected at y = -0.30 ± 0.05. The front keeps y < -0.25, everything ahead of the throat wall at -0.55;
  the back keeps y > -0.35.
- The open loops of each cut are capped: 2,317 cap faces on the front half, 2,372 on the back.
- Front 396,912 tris kept of 699,697; back 396,064 of 699,796.
- The two halves are joined with both TRELLIS textures (`Material_0`, `Material_0.001`) into a 73,829,332-byte GLB,
  sha256 `5571783e3b358f17a7e6a0073e7664bc1ee6cdf8165906cd45b7c601ec902546`, in 13.9 s.

**Watertight.** finish.py's voxel remesh is the merge. The record's 1 cm voxel trial of the join gave 710,284 tris, 4
components and 0 open or non-manifold edges. The finished card (below) is 1 component and watertight.

**Two cuts were tried:**

- try1: cut -0.10, `<out>/renders/merge1.png`;
- try2: cut -0.30, `merge2.png`.

Both close the back. try2 was kept because it takes more of the closed back and less of the s7's torn flank, with the
same front.

**Tests.** `python -m unittest apps/vr/tools/mesh/test_merge_halves.py`, 15 tests on `halves_lib.py`:

- the turn is never a reflection;
- the percentile footprint ignores a flap;
- the back takes the front's height and footprint;
- rotation comes before the fit;
- the seam masks overlap by twice the band;
- argument refusals.
