## 1. Why the ring read as rope

`SpellVfx::PerfectRingPuffs` placed 20 beads on a ring that widens from 8 to 26 cm. Each bead was a streak card lying along
the tangent, `Bead x 2.4 x RingCardScale` long: 6 x 2.4 x 1.7 = 24.5 cm at age 0 (Fire x 1.3 = 31.8 cm), against 2.5 cm of
arc per bead at radius 8 cm. So every card covered about ten neighbours, and the heads and tails merged into one twisted
band. `T75-before/shots/06-fire-perfect.png` shows it (a braided wreath). The streak cells (13 droplet, 14 spark) are a
round head at +U with a tail behind it. The head is already a bead; the tail and the spacing made the rope.

## 2. The change (0f9d524, code only)

- **No re-render.** The atlas is full (16 of 16 cells), so a new bead cell would replace another cell. M_SpellGlow already
  maps U = u x d0 + d1 from the card's custom data, so a card can show only part of a cell's width. `FPuff` gained
  `CropU0` / `CropU1` (default 0 / 1, so every other card's custom data is bit-identical). `CellOfCustomData` reads the
  column under the middle of the part shown.
- **The bead** is the head of the school's streak with its tail cropped off: the droplet's head with its whole dark rim
  (cell width 0.55-0.92), the spark's lit disc with its glow (0.58-0.96). The ranges were measured on the atlas alpha.
  Fire keeps its spark head, and Water and Earth their droplet head. Air's wisp has no round head, so Air uses the droplet
  head in Air's colour. The card is as tall as the whole cell, so the head is not stretched: the visible bead is about 1:1
  (droplet head 44 x 42 px in the cell), on a card of 0.37 : 1.
- **Spacing:** `RingBeads` = 10. Each bead's card, clear border included, spans `RingBeadShare` = 0.5 of the chord to its
  neighbour at age 0 (Fire x `FireStreakScale` 1.3). It shrinks to `RingBeadEnd` = 0.5 of that at age 1. The ring's radius
  curve (8 to 26 cm) and every colour are unchanged.

`MageArena.SpellVfx.PerfectRingBeadsApart` measures each bead's length as its card's on-screen width (the clear border
counted as bead, so it errs on the safe side) against the distance between neighbouring centres:

| Gap / bead | age 0 | age 0.5 | age 0.9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (T67), fire | -0.93 | -0.71 | -0.38 |
| before (T67), water / air / earth | -0.91 | -0.62 | -0.20 |
| **T75, fire** | **0.54** | **1.05** | **1.80** |
| **T75, water / air / earth** | **1.00** | **1.67** | **2.64** |

The bar was a gap of at least 0.5 of a bead.

**Verdict on the bar:** the ruling's point, beads not rope, was met in 3 of 3 rounds for every school. The bar also needs
the ring to still read as the perfect-absorb reward in its school's colour, and never as a threat. T75 got "weakly" on the
reward in 9 of 9 cells, and two rounds read the water beads as a water bolt ("yes"). By the letter the bar failed, so the
card's fallback ran. `73a5e28` reverts `0f9d524` and `502e4ab`, the test with them since it is red on today's ring by
design, and `git diff d5f584d -- apps/vr/Game/Source` is empty.

**Why it failed** (the same reasons in all three rounds):
- **It does not read as a ring.** Ten beads with wide gaps only imply a circle, and at 8 m the circle is gone.
- **The splash competes with it.** The unchanged splash throws ten droplets or sparks of about the same size at the same
  moment, so beads and splash merge into one "burst" or "firework".
- **A single round ball in an element colour is a bolt's shape** in this game's threat language. The water beads' glossy
  droplet shading made that worst. The rope read was a problem of the band; the reward read and the threat separation came
  from the band too.

## 7. Open points (for the owner)

- **A necklace, not a scatter.** The gap the test asks for (half a bead) is far smaller than T75's at the capture age
  (0.22, where the ring is 15 cm across and the gap is about 1.25 beads, Fire 0.73; 2.64 by age 0.9). A denser ring keeps the beads apart
  and lets the circle close. Examples: 16-20 beads at a share of 0.6-0.65, or beads that grow with the radius instead of
  shrinking. Recommended as the next try, with the same test bar and blind bar.
- **Separate the ring from the splash.** On a perfect absorb, draw the splash's droplets smaller or fewer, or later than
  the ring (the crown is already dropped there). `SplashPuffs` was frozen for this lane.
- **Keep the beads off the bolt's shape.** A bead with a glow that is not a full sphere (the spark head's soft disc read
  less bolt-like than the glossy droplet), or beads joined by a faint thread (a beaded string, not a rope), would keep the
  reward read. A thread needs a new cell, so a re-render.
- **Read on its spirit:** the ruling's own words, "beads, not rope", were met 3 of 3. If the owner rules that "reward" and
  "threat" are separate bars, `git cherry-pick 502e4ab 0f9d524` restores T75 with its test.
- **Quest truth** is unmeasured: how 3-4 cm beads at 1.1 m read through the Quest's lenses and mips.
