**Material** (`M_Crowd_Atlas`), per pixel, `atlas` the sampled colour at the rect UV:

```
shade   = saturate(luminance(atlas) / 0.20)            a tunic never exceeds its tint
weight  = saturate((fraction down the figure - 0.22) / 0.10)   0 on the head and raised arms
colour  = lerp(lerp(atlas, tint * shade, weight), tint, flat)
opacity = lerp(atlas alpha, 1, flat)
```

The fraction down the figure is the sampled v between the rect's top and bottom v floats (either order, `Min`/`Max`), so it
holds for every variant and for both V orientations. A banner (flat = 1) is the tint at full opacity; the atlas never
shows. Roughness 1, specular 0, masked at 0.5, one-sided, default lit: unchanged.

## 3. The threat-colour guard

The tints are chosen in `Greybox/ArenaStands.cpp:114` from a search over chroma and luminance (a scratch script, not
committed), then held by `MageArena.Greybox.Crowd.TintNotThreat` in the greybox palette and styles 13, 25 and 30. For each
tint and each palette:

| Check | Bound |
|---|---|
| Luminance | under the dimmest threat colour (water, fire, air, earth, steel, rim; style 25's steel, 0.0560, is the least) |
| Saturation | under the least saturated chromatic threat (0.765, the greybox air; 0.837 in style 30) |
| Chroma distance | at least `BrazierFire::ThreatChromaMargin` (0.15) from all seven threat colours, by `ArenaCrowd::ChromaDistance` (linear RGB over its brightest channel, the T60/T61 method) |
| Black core class | brightest channel at least 0.08, so no tint is a black core |

Tint luminances are 0.054 (banner 0.0525), saturations 0.48-0.70, least chroma distance 0.198 (brick, to style 25's earth)
and 0.243 for the banner. The shade never exceeds 1, so the drawn albedo is at most the tint.

**Two readings of the ruling, flagged for Athena.** (1) "The dimmest threat colour" would be the unblockable's black core,
under which no colour can sit; the core is the leave cue's dark half, so the luminance bound excludes it and the core is
guarded by the brightest-channel floor and by chroma. (2) "The least saturated threat colour" would be steel (saturation 0
in the greybox); steel and the core are the achromatic classes, so the saturation bound is over the chromatic five and steel
and the core are guarded by chroma. Steel stays in the luminance bound.

**Negative controls.** The test also asserts that the checks fire on an over-bright orange `(0.60, 0.20, 0.05)`, an
over-saturated red and a steel grey, under every palette. And the real red run: with the rust tint set to
`(0.60, 0.20, 0.05)`, `Crowd.TintNotThreat` failed with, for the greybox palette, `luminance 0.2742 is not under the
dimmest threat's 0.1695`, `saturation 0.917 is not under ... 0.765`, `stands 0.076 off fire by chroma` and `0.141 off earth`,
and the same four lines under styles 13, 25 and 30 (`<out>/tests/tint-red/automation.log`, exit 255). The file was
restored, touched and rebuilt before everything that follows.

**Helpers.** `BrazierFire.h` exports only `ThreatChromaMargin` and `MinBrightest`; `Chroma` and `ChromaDistance` live in
the test files' anonymous namespaces (`BrazierTests.cpp`, `SpellVfxTests.cpp`). The test reuses the margin; `Chroma` and
`ChromaDistance` are new in `ArenaCrowd` because `BrazierFire.h` is outside this card's paths. Folding the three copies into
one is a follow-up.

  mis-numbered its per-pair headings (it says so and tells the reader to go by file name); its three overall verdicts match
  the first's and the individual descriptions match the stills.
- **The watch item holds.** The ember flecks over the crowd band stay clearly orange; against the tinted right-hand stands
  they have "slightly less contrast". The tints are darker than the clay they replace (tunic albedo 0.054 luminance at most,

  white, green or skin), the banners are tall dark slabs rather than trimmed pennants on the wall face, no torches or
  warm rim-light on the crowd, and the palette is muddy. The muted constraint holds that last one in place by design.
