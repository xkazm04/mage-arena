## 3. Decisions the card did not specify

1. **Batching: option (b), one ISM of one quad with custom data, not (a) one baked static mesh.** Both are one draw per
   pass; I measured (b). (a) was the first attempt: building a mesh in UE needs `FMeshDescription`, and build 1 failed at
   link time on its exported mutators (`CreateVertexInstance_Internal`, `CreateTriangle_Internal`,
   `FStaticMeshAttributes::Register`): `MageArenaVR.Build.cs` does not link `MeshDescription`, and it is outside this
   card's paths (DRAWCALL-BUDGET hit the same wall). (b) is also the better fit on its own terms: the placement stays live
   from the layout at runtime (a baked mesh would freeze it, and go stale when the stands change), the per-card vertex
   cost is the same 4 vertices, and the instance count stays testable. Risk for Quest: per-instance custom data on the
   mobile path is not measured here (no headset); the material falls back to the whole atlas if it were missing.
2. **The card is the engine's 1 m plane** (`/Engine/BasicShapes/Plane`), not a new mesh asset, and the four A07 FBX cards
   are not imported. The plane's U runs along +X and V along +Y (`Crowd.Card` measures it from the render data). The
   instance turns the plane's normal to the centre and its U to the viewer's right; the custom data orders the rect's
   top and bottom to match its V, so no mesh is mirrored and the front face stays front.
3. **C3 (mirroring):** not exercised as an FBX check, because no FBX is imported. The atlas itself reads correctly: in the
   after stills StandingPoint points to the viewer's right and no figure is mirrored (`<out>/probe-crop-right.png`).
   No card defect was found, so nothing goes back to Lynx.
4. **436 cards, not 470.** `placement.json` targets the kit's treads, which do not exist; the greybox ring at the
   0.55 m pitch holds about 500, and four diagonal aisles (one wall segment each, mirrored across both axes, like S1's stairs
   and crowd.py's empty stair aisles) leave 436.
5. **Mid-tread rows.** Each greybox tier is one 1.55 m tread, so one row per tier, on its middle, as crowd.py placed one row
   per kit tread.
6. **Facing the arena centre point,** as the brief says, not the tier normal; on an oval the two differ away from the axes.
7. **The report is this doc plus `runs/T57/REPORT.md`** as the card asks. `runs/` is never committed (repo law), so the
   runs file is local evidence; this doc is the record.
