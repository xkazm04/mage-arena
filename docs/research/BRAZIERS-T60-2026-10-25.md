# T60 braziers as fire props, 2026-10-25

**Label: desktop proxy (RTX 4090, D3D12, forward shading, MSAA 4x, `-nohmd`), not Quest truth.** Engine UE 5.8.3 (CL
58210709; `Build.version` read before the first build). No combat number moved: nothing under `apps/vr/data/` changed
(`arena-layout.json` included: no bowl-shape field was needed).

**Outcome.**

- **The bowl reads as a bowl.** Each brazier is a broad foot, a slim stem and five equal rings on a rounded profile
  (radius 0.44, 0.71, 0.87, 0.96, 1.0 of the layout's bowl radius), all on the brazier's existing instanced cylinder
  component: 28 instances instead of 8, still one draw per pass, shadows as before. Every blind critic round picked it
  over the old disc in every pair ("a flared bowl with a lip, a waisted stem and a foot, against a flat puck on a post").
- **The flame is no longer a threat colour.** The old cone was the style's `projectiles.fire`, the fire spell's absorb
  colour (the greybox `colours.flame` was the same orange); every critic round read every old image as YES on (b) ("the
  cone is the same saturated ember orange as the fire bolt"). The new flame is an environment colour of its own and every
  round read every new image as NO.
- **One instanced flame layer per colour, two for all four braziers.** Five puffs a brazier (a bed in the bowl's mouth, a
  tall tongue, a side tongue, a low heart and a heart tip), 12 body and 8 heart instances of the engine sphere, drawn
  with `M_SpellGlow` (no new asset). In the D-G4 dumps they are exactly two `TranslucencyAfterDOF` rows
  (`MID_M_SpellGlow`, 12 and 8 instances, 19,200 tris).
- **A slow, broad lick, not sparks.** Each puff breathes and leans about its own place on three incommensurate waves
  (0.55, 0.93 and 1.4 Hz) with a phase per brazier; nothing rises. T58's ember trail flickers at about 3.7 Hz and its
  embers climb behind the bolt.
- **Optional.** `-MageArenaNoBrazierFire` leaves the bowls cold (`MAGEVR_ARENA_BRAZIER_FIRE off
  (-MageArenaNoBrazierFire)`; on: `MAGEVR_ARENA_BRAZIER_FIRE braziers=4 puffs=20 components=2`).
- **What it did not reach:** the flame does not read as fire. The colour rule (dimmer than every threat glow) puts it
  below the lit wall behind it, and all three critics saw smoke, a dry shrub or a khaki candle flame. Section 6.

## 3. Decisions the card did not specify

1. **CPU sway, not a material flicker.** The puffs are re-posed each frame by the greybox actor (20 transforms, one
   `BatchUpdateInstancesTransforms` per layer); `M_SpellGlow` is reused as is, with `GlowGain` 1.4 on the flame's two
   instances. No new material, no commandlet run, no `.uasset`. The actor ticks only while the flames exist.
2. **Two layers, not one:** a body and a brighter heart give the flame a core, at the brief's upper bound of two
   components.
3. **"Dimmer than any threat glow" is luminance against the dimmest of the four element colours and the rim** of the
   palette in force (the rim is the binding one: 0.169 greybox, 0.090 style 25). Steel never glows, so it is held off by
   chroma only.
4. **The chroma margin is 0.15** (in linear RGB divided by its brightest channel), well outside the 0.02 class tolerance
   of `SpellVfx.ColoursKeepThreatClass`. Earth is the nearest class (0.18-0.31).
5. **The flame stays inside the old cone's silhouette.** From the seat, the near-right brazier stands on the demo
   unblockable's flight line in `04-threats-midflight`; any flame there hides part of the rim. The test holds the flame to
   the cone's envelope, so it never hides more than the shipped cone did.
6. **`arena-layout.json` is untouched.** Its `colours.flame` (0.95, 0.35, 0.06) is no longer read (the loader no longer
   asks for it), and its `_propNote` still says "cone flame". Removing the field is a data edit the card did not need;
   it is left for the next layout change.
7. **Evidence of every round is kept**, not only the last (`stills/greybox-after{1..5}`, `vfx-after{1..3}`).

## 6. Open, for Athena

- **The flame does not read as fire, and inside this colour rule it cannot.** At the desktop proxy's exposure the lit
  wall and sand behind the braziers sit at about the luminance of the dimmest threat glow (the rim, 0.169), so a flame
  held below it reads as smoke or a dry plant. Every critic suggested "a pale warm-white or yellow core ... kept clearly
  lighter and less saturated than the fire bolt". That breaks "lower luminance than any threat glow" but keeps the
  chroma rule. Options: (A) keep the rule and the dim flame (shipped now; the bowl alone is the gain); (B) let the heart
  rise above the rim's luminance while staying below Fire's and Earth's (0.38 / 0.31 greybox), with a pale, desaturated
  chroma held off every threat class by the same test; (C) give the flame light instead of colour (an emissive bowl rim
  or a small sand light pool, measured against the draw budget). Recommendation: (B), a one-constant change
  (`HeartShare` against the elements only), re-checked by a blind critic.
- **The near-right brazier at (-2, -7.2) stands on the demo unblockable's flight line from the centre seat.** Any
  flame there hides part of the rim. The new flame hides less than the cone, but moving that brazier (a layout change)
  would clear it.
- **Quest truth.** The +2 translucent layers, the 20 extra bowl cylinders in two shadow cascades and the per-frame
  instance updates are desktop numbers; the device measure is still owed.
- **`arena-layout.json`** still carries the unread `colours.flame` and a "cone flame" note (section 3.6).
