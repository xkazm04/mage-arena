## The image against the front (my reading, not a critic's)

- **Closed and whole:** yes. The back is one continuous warty shell from the top of the back to the haunches; no tear, hole,
  gap or cut-away anywhere, and no gape, teeth or throat leak through from the front (the thing the prompt guarded against).
- **Palette and finish:** the same mud-brown hide, darker brown lumps and tan underside on the same flat light grey with no
  cast shadow. The hide reads a little more orange-saturated than the master, and its lumps are rounder and more numerous
  (the master's are fewer, larger and more conical).
- **Proportions and scale:** close but not identical. The back is about 11 % narrower in the frame (long side 1,076 px against
  1,214) because a rear view of a squat body is narrower than a front three-quarter view. The head is almost hidden: only two
  small yellow eyes show at the top edge, and the head does not read as a distinct wide head merged into the body as in the
  front. The rear legs are thick, bowed and clawed as the front's, and the front feet show beyond the flanks.
- **Faults to weigh:** it is a near-symmetrical blob with little landmark detail, so TRELLIS gets a good closed silhouette
  but few cues about where the head ends. The body height is about the master's but the squat is a touch lower. There is no
  tail and the master has none either. The view is dead-on behind, while the master is a front three-quarter: the two do not
  share an exact camera, so the multi-view node's 0 and 180 degree azimuths are an approximation.
- Net: a usable back that fixes the torn-shell problem on paper; whether the mesh improves is what the TRELLIS run tests.

## The two-view TRELLIS.2 path (built and tested, not run)

- **What the installed pack offers** (read on disk, `C:/Users/kazda/ai-tools/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_trellis2.py`; ComfyUI
  not started): `Trellis2Conditioning` (one image) and `Pixal3DMultiViewConditioning` (views `front`, `left`, `back`,
  `right` at azimuth 0 / 90 / 180 / 270, `fov` default 20, each view 1024 square with the object spanning about 1/1.1 of the
  frame, any subset connected). That is the pack's only multi-image node and it is the one used: front at 0 and back at 180.
- **Caveat that matters:** the installed checkpoint `trellis_2_int8_convrot.safetensors` has **no `proj_linear` weights**
  (checked in its safetensors header: 0 keys), so ComfyUI's model detection leaves it in the "global" attention mode. In that
  mode the node's only effect on the model is the mean of the two views' DINOv3 class and register tokens (`global_512` and
  `global_1024`); the per-voxel feature projection that `Pixal3D` checkpoints use is computed and then ignored. The back view
  therefore steers the whole-object conditioning, it does not project geometry onto the back of the mesh. Whether that is
  enough to stop the torn back is unproven; this is the cheapest path the installed weights allow, and a real Pixal3D
  checkpoint would be a separate download (a spend and licence question, not made here). The NAF upsampling the node also
  runs costs extra VRAM and time over the single-image job.
- **Code:** `apps/vr/tools/mesh/trellis-mv.mjs` builds the workflow from `apps/vr/tools/assets/trellis-api.json` (unchanged):
  it clones the front's view chain (LoadImage 122, RemoveBackground 192, switch 248, MaskPreview 303, ImageCropToMask 312, pad
  1.1) as nodes 422, 492, 448, 403, 412 for the back, adds node 399 `Pixal3DMultiViewConditioning` (front 312, back 412, fov 20)
  and rewires every reader of node 299 to it. `comfy.mjs run` takes `--back <image>` (routes to `trellis-mv.mjs`) and
  `--dry-run` (prints the workflow, before the guard: no ComfyUI, no GPU, no upload). Tests: `node --test
  apps/vr/tools/mesh/trellis-mv.test.mjs` (6 tests: arguments, the one-view graph unchanged, the two-view wiring, every wire
  resolves, upload names, the CLI dry run end to end). Documented in `apps/vr/tools/mesh/README.md`.
- **The dry run** (`... run <front> maw-mv 7 <outdir> --back <back> --dry-run`) prints a 59-node graph with two `LoadImage`
  nodes (`t38_maw-mv_front.jpg`, `t38_maw-mv_back.jpg`) feeding node 399.
