01-water-bolt

Screen-Print Poster · 01-water-bolt

Prompt

Style: vintage screen-print poster, flat bold shapes, four colours only plus paper, visible halftone dots in the shadows, slight misregistration, strong graphic composition, sun disc and long shadows, heroic simplified figures. A single small water projectile in flight across an arena, seen from a seated first-person view: a tight turquoise orb with a short swirling tail and droplets, crisp silhouette against the background, clearly readable at a distance of eight metres. No characters except the casting hand at the lower edge. No text, no letters, no numbers.

02-ward-absorb

Screen-Print Poster · 02-ward-absorb

Prompt

Style: vintage screen-print poster, flat bold shapes, four colours only plus paper, visible halftone dots in the shadows, slight misregistration, strong graphic composition, sun disc and long shadows, heroic simplified figures. First-person view of a raised left palm projecting a curved translucent water shield shaped like a 140-degree arc in front of the player; an incoming vermilion fire bolt hits the shield and is swallowed in a ring-shaped splash and ripple across the arc.
