sigil-mudra

Both index tips move from rest into the seal. Left hand first, then the right. The pose is not a $Q class.

Projected from mudra.normal.jsonl with the stroke builder's plane. 23 left samples and 23 right samples after the hold is trimmed to one endpoint, then resampled to 64 points.

The thin red line is the projected anchor template (or, for the mudra, the unsmoothed clip). Ink is the canonical stroke.
