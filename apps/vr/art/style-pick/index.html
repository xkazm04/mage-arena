Style pick: one duel in four skins (T16, P18)

The same scripted duel against Brennic (bout 2, seed 1, the reference seated script) was recorded four times at 1920x1080 and 60 fps, one sim tick per frame. The four runs differ only in the -MageArenaStyle flag. Each style recolours the greybox shapes with its sampled colours (apps/vr/data/vr/styles/style-<id>.json): sand, stands, wall, dais, pads, seats, markers, braziers, banners, hands and the sky dome, plus its threat palette. The sky, threats and bodies draw unlit. The arena shell keeps the greybox's lit material under a neutral light of the greybox's brightness. Bodies, the wrist cuff and the HP bar keep the greybox colours in every skin. The threat language holds in all four: magic is a ring in its element colour, steel is angular, and only the unblockable is a black core with a red rim. All four event logs are byte-identical (203 events, md5 7b0715c871ac3399504c4f7a744896d4), so the skins change pixels, never the simulation.

The pick is the owner's: 13, 25 or 30, recorded in the owner's words in docs/DECISIONS.md.

Videos (31.17 s, 1870 frames each)

Greybox

The baseline: no -MageArenaStyle flag, the dusk greybox the mechanics were built in. Here for comparison, not a candidate.

13 Screen-Print Poster

Kept from its study: the poster inks on the arena. Ochre sand, a brick-red wall, ink-dark dais, seats and braziers, red banners, and an olive-gold sky over a teal zenith. Not shown yet: the ink outline, halftone and misregistration (texture work).

25 Moonlit Silver Nocturne

Kept from its study: the night palette. A navy sky, cold blue-grey stands, a dark slate-blue wall and pale moonlit sand, so the turquoise and ember threats are the only saturated colours on screen. Not shown yet: the silver rim light and glow (lighting work).

30 Chalk & Slate

Kept from its study: the slate board. A slate sky, a slate-grey wall, chalk-pale stands, warm chalk-coloured sand and navy banners. Not shown yet: the chalk-white outlines and the hand-drawn line texture.
